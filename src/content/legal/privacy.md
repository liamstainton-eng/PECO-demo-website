## Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of your personal information (access)
- ask us to correct anything that is wrong (rectification)
- ask us to delete it (erasure)
- ask us to limit how we use it (restriction)
- object to us using it based on our legitimate interests (objection)
- ask for it in a portable format where it applies (portability)

These rights are not absolute, and some only apply in certain situations. To use any of them, contact us using the details below. We will normally reply within one month.
