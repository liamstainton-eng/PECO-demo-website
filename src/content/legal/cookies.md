## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website saves on your computer or phone when you visit it. They can help a site work properly, stay secure or remember your choices.

## Cookies this site uses

This site does not set any cookies of its own. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set one strictly necessary cookie.

| Name      | Purpose                                                                                                                                           | Expires    |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| `__cf_bm` | May be set by Cloudflare. It helps Cloudflare's bot management tell real visitors from automated traffic, so the site stays secure and available. | 30 minutes |

Strictly necessary cookies do not need your consent.

## Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to the site. Cloudflare describes it as cookieless and says it does not collect personal data. Because it sets no cookies, we do not ask for your consent. See our [privacy notice](/privacy) for more.

## Advertising and tracking

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Google Maps is a plain link to another site, not an embed, so this site sets no Google cookies. If you follow the link, Google's own cookie policy applies.

## How to manage cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies in their settings. Blocking all cookies may stop some sites working properly. For step-by-step guidance, see [aboutcookies.org](https://www.aboutcookies.org).
